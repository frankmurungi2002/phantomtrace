Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data PhantomTrace collects, why we collect it, how we store it, and the rights you have over it. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is still unclear, email mywatchhhh@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

PhantomTrace is operated by Francis Murungi, Software Engineering, Mbarara University of Science and Technology, Uganda. For privacy questions, including data access and deletion requests, write to mywatchhhh@gmail.com.

2. What we collect, and why

Category Examples Why we need it
Account Full name, email, phone number, hashed password, two-factor code seed (if enabled) To create and secure your account.
Devices Laptop hostname, operating system version, MAC and IP addresses, CPU model, disk sizes, running process list, laptop manufacturer and model (for BIOS instructions) To identify your device, show it in your dashboard, and prove ownership in a recovery report.
Location Latitude, longitude, Wi-Fi access points seen nearby, inferred city and country To help you locate the device if it is missing.
Evidence Webcam photos and screenshots captured ONLY when you (the owner) send the corresponding command, or when the device is marked STOLEN To identify the person using the device and support a police report.
Emergency contacts Names, emails, phone numbers you add for people to notify when a device is marked stolen So we can email them the recovery report if you ask.
Technical logs Heartbeat timestamps, command history, error messages, agent and app version To diagnose problems and improve reliability.
We DO NOT:
  • Capture webcam photos or screenshots without an explicit command or without the device being marked STOLEN by you.
  • Record audio.
  • Log your keystrokes.
  • Read the contents of your personal files.
  • Sell your data to anyone.
  • Use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account record, device metadata, heartbeat log, command history, location points, and emergency contacts are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by our cloud-infrastructure provider. Webcam photos and screenshots are stored in a dedicated image-hosting provider. All providers are reputable third parties under contractual obligations to protect data. Specific provider names are listed in the Sub-processor list and may change as we evolve the service; the categories of data we store remain as described above.

4. Who can see your data

You

You can see all of your own data inside the mobile app or by downloading a recovery report.

Emergency contacts you have added

When you mark a device STOLEN, each emergency contact with an email on file receives the current recovery report (one email per mark-stolen event). They do not get continuing access.

PhantomTrace operator

The operator (Francis Murungi) has administrative access to the database for the purpose of maintaining the service and responding to support requests. The operator will not access the contents of individual accounts (including evidence images) unless you ask for support that requires it, or unless legally compelled.

Infrastructure providers

Our hosting, database, image-storage, SMS, email, and push-notification providers process your data only as needed to deliver the service. They are bound by contract and by their own privacy practices. See the sub-processor list for the current providers.

Law enforcement

We disclose data to law enforcement only in response to a valid legal process issued under Ugandan law. We will notify you unless the order prohibits notice.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Account data — for as long as your account exists.
  • Device heartbeats, command history, location points — for 180 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Evidence (photos, screenshots) — for 90 days from capture, then deleted automatically. You can delete individual items sooner from the Evidence screen.
  • Recovery reports — generated on demand, not stored server-side.
  • Account deletion — within 30 days of your request, all of your personal data is removed from our active systems; backup copies are purged within an additional 60 days.

6. Security

We use industry-standard protections:

  • Passwords are stored as salted bcrypt hashes, never plain text.
  • All network traffic between the app, the agent, and the server uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Two-factor authentication (TOTP) and biometric sign-in are available on the mobile app.
  • Devices marked STOLEN trigger automatic USB-storage lockdown and BitLocker enforcement where supported.

No system is perfectly secure. If we detect a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you by email within 72 hours and advise on steps to protect yourself.

7. Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (download via the app)
  • Correct inaccurate data (edit via the Settings screen)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Object to processing you believe is unlawful
  • Lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Office of Uganda (pdpo.go.ug)

To exercise these rights, use the controls in the app or email mywatchhhh@gmail.com.

8. Children

PhantomTrace is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has signed up for the service, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. International transfers

Our infrastructure providers may store or process your data outside Uganda, including in Europe or North America. We only use providers that commit to protection standards comparable to those required by Ugandan law.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes we will notify you in the app and by email at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
