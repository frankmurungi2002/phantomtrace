1. What PhantomTrace is

PhantomTrace is a laptop anti-theft and recovery platform. It consists of:

  • A Windows background agent that runs on your laptop(s)
  • A mobile app that lets you monitor and control those laptops
  • A cloud backend that relays commands and stores evidence

The service helps you locate a stolen laptop, capture evidence of who is using it (webcam photos, screenshots), lock the screen, encrypt your files, and share a recovery report with police.
